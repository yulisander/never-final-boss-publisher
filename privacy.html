Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Never Final Boss Publisher is a private desktop tool operated by the owner of the Never Final Boss channel. It uses TikTok authorization solely to identify the owner's account and upload or publish the owner's original video content.

Information processed

The tool may process basic profile information returned by TikTok, authorization tokens, upload status, and publication identifiers required to perform requested publishing operations.

Storage and sharing

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's protected Linux computer. They are not placed in this repository, sold, shared with advertisers, or disclosed to unrelated third parties. The tool does not offer accounts to the public and does not collect data from third-party users.

Retention and control

Authorization information is retained only while needed to operate the owner's publishing workflow. The owner may revoke access through TikTok account settings and delete locally stored credentials at any time.

Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the public repository's issue tracker: GitHub Issues.
